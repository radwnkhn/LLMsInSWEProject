## What changed

- **`backend/app/metrics.py`.**
  - The four targets and fail lines: 80/60, 70/50, 60/40 and 50/30.
  - The status rule: on target at or above the target, failing below the fail line, below target in between,
    and no data with nothing to count.
  - The calculations:
    - Trainer agreement: "correct" out of graded sets; "partly correct" doesn't count.
    - Helpful ratings: ratings of 4 or 5 out of all ratings.
    - Cue tried: "yes" out of yes and no; "not yet" isn't counted.
    - 7-day return: lifters who uploaded again within 7 days of their first upload.
- **Repository.** `list_feedback_between` and `list_graded_verdicts` cover analyses created in a range.
  `list_upload_times` returns every upload's owner and time. Both in memory and in Postgres. No migration was
  needed.
- **API.** `GET /admin/metrics?start=&end=` uses the same inclusive UTC date range as the cost and usage
  reports (the last 30 days by default; bad or reversed dates get 422). It returns the four metrics with their
  value, hits, count, a "3 graded sets" basis, target, fail line and status, plus `pending_returns`. Admins only.
- **Frontend.** `/admin/metrics`, linked from the Analyses admin page, has a From/To filter. It shows one card
  per metric: the percentage (or "—"), the target and fail line, a colored status label, and "Based on
  3 graded sets (2 agreed)". The 7-day return card also says how many lifters are still in their first week.

## Assumptions

- **Which date counts.** Grades, ratings and cue answers count by the date the analysis was created. That way
  a range means "sets analyzed in this period", and grading or rating them later doesn't move them to another
  period.
- **7-day return.** A lifter's first upload must fall in the range and be at least 7 days old. Another upload
  within 7 days after it (inclusive) counts as coming back. Lifters still in their first week are reported but
  not counted, so the share isn't pulled down by people who haven't had the chance to return. If a lifter's
  first upload was deleted, their earliest remaining upload counts as their first.
- **Deleted data.** Uploads, ratings and grades a user or moderator deleted are gone (US-21, US-27), so they
  aren't counted.
- **No data isn't 0%.** With nothing to count, a metric shows "—" and "No data", never a failing 0%.
