# US-28: More lifts (overhead press and Romanian deadlift)

Issue: [#30](https://github.com/radwnkhn/LLMsInSWEProject/issues/30).
Branch `beta/us-28-more-lifts` starts from `origin/main` (`75b926a`), which includes US-25 (the fault list),
US-26 and US-27. Scope: exactly two new lifts, `overhead_press` ("Overhead Press") and `romanian_deadlift`
("Romanian Deadlift").

## Where a lift lives in the pipeline

Before writing anything, the existing lifts were traced through the app. Each place a new lift must be added:

- **`backend/app/lifts.py`:** the lift type, `LIFTS`, labels and nouns. A new `with_article()` gives
  "an overhead press" in validator messages.
- **`backend/app/measure.py`:**
  - Rep signals: the overhead press uses wrists above shoulders, resting low with the bar racked. The
    Romanian deadlift uses the hip-hinge angle, with a 20° minimum.
  - The per-lift metrics.
  - A backward-lean series for the press.
  - Overhead detection in `detect_lift()` and `analysis_lift`.
- **`backend/app/faults.py`:** three faults per lift, plus their measurements in US-25's `METRICS`, which
  admins pick from when adding a fault.
- **`backend/app/comparison.py`:** the RDL gets the knee and hip rows in the US-20 comparison table.
- **`backend/app/llm/schema.py`, `prompt.py`, `main.py`:** these listed the three lifts by hand.
- **`backend/app/testing/synthetic.py` and `fakes.py`:** side-view poses for both lifts, test-mode scenarios
  (`ohp…`, `rdl…`) and cues for the mocked LLM.
- **`supabase/migrations/20261114000000_us28_more_lifts.sql`:** every table that stores a lift (`uploads`,
  `analyses`, `faults`, `moderation_log`) accepts the new lifts, and the six faults are seeded into the
  US-25 fault list.
- **Frontend:** the `Lift` type, `lifts.ts`, `filming.ts`, two example frames in `public/framing/`, and the
  lift picker's grid, which now wraps to two columns on a phone.

These needed no change because they are generic over the fault catalog: `rules.py`, `validator.py`
(including safety-first ordering), `correction.py`, `analyzability.py`, `breakdown.py`, `annotate.py`,
`results.py`, `history.py`, and the admin pages, whose lift filters loop over `LIFTS`.

### Tests changed during the green phase

- **Overhead press geometry.** The red tests used 0.15 + 0.15 arm links, which puts the hands at lockout
  above the top of the frame (y = -0.1). The synthetic press now uses 0.11 + 0.11 links and stands at
  y = 0.95, so the whole press is in frame. The three affected hand calculations were reworked:
  - lockout height: D = 0.22 sin(θ/2);
  - rack elbow angle: 2 asin(0.05/0.22) = 26.3°, previously 19.2°.
- **`us28_%s_filming_guide_has_its_own_example_frame`** built the alt text as "a " plus the lowercased
  label ("a overhead press"). It now accepts "a" or "an" and ignores case.
- **The US-25 browser test** found the section by the name "Deadlift faults", which, not being an exact
  match, now also matches "Romanian Deadlift faults". It now uses exact names, and its check of the fault
  page covers all five lifts.
- **One app change, caught by an existing test.** US-03's per-lift tests, which now also run for the new
  lifts, require every guide to say "hip height". The first overhead press guide said "chest height". It
  was changed to hip height, which still fits the bar at lockout from 3 m, rather than changing US-03's
  criterion.

## Thresholds (first guesses)

Every value below is a starting point, to be tuned with trainer grading (US-22) and threshold editing
(US-24). None has been checked against real video yet.

| Lift | Fault | Measurement | First guess | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overhead press | Lower back arches (**safety**) | Backward lean of the hip-to-shoulder line, `torso_lean_back_max_deg` | above 15° | A strict press keeps the torso within about 5–10° of vertical, with the lifter leaning back just enough for the bar to clear the face. 15° leaves room for that and flags clear layback, which mostly comes from arching the lower back. |
| Overhead press | Bar drifts forward | Wrist forward/back range, `bar_drift_ratio` | above 0.30 thigh lengths | The bar must travel from in front of the throat to over the shoulders: 0.20 in the clean synthetic press, about 5–10 cm in real presses. 0.30 is looser than the squat and bench (0.25), so only drift beyond that path is flagged. |
| Overhead press | Incomplete lockout | Largest elbow angle in the rep, `lockout_elbow_angle_deg` | below 165° | The same value as the deadlift hip lockout, slightly stricter than the bench's 160°. Overhead, the elbows should finish nearly straight, and MediaPipe's elbow angle at full lockout typically reads 170–180°. |
| RDL | Rounded back (**safety**) | Ear-shoulder-hip bend, `back_rounding_deg` | above 20° | The same measurement and value as the deadlift's rounded back, since the hinge loads the spine the same way. |
| RDL | Knees bend too much | Smallest knee angle, `knee_angle_min_deg` | below 140° | An RDL keeps a soft knee, about 150–165°. A conventional deadlift bends to about 100–120° at the floor, so below 140° means the lift has turned into a deadlift. |
| RDL | Bar drifts away from the legs | Wrist forward/back range, `bar_drift_ratio` | above 0.15 thigh lengths | The same as the deadlift. The bar should slide down the thighs and stay over the midfoot. |

Rep counting also has first-guess values:
- **RDL minimum hinge:** a rep must hinge at least 20° at the hip.
- **Overhead detection:** the wrists must be at least half a torso length above the shoulders on at least
  10% of frames.

## Still needs real labeled videos

The synthetic data shows the code computes what was intended. It can't show that the measurements and
thresholds match real lifters. Before relying on these lifts, the team should record and label (the US-06
labeled-video fixtures, `backend/tests/fixtures/labeled_videos/labels.json`):

- **Rep counts.** For each new lift: several sets of 3–8 reps from the recommended side view, with the true
  rep count, to check rep counting on real pose noise. For the RDL, include sets with a short pause at the
  bottom and sets that touch the floor.
- **Faults.** For each fault: sets with trainer-marked reps where it happens and where it doesn't, so US-22
  grading can tune the thresholds above:
  - press: layback, a forward bar path, soft lockouts;
  - RDL: rounding, deep knee bend, the bar drifting forward.
- **Facing both ways.** Overhead presses filmed facing left and facing right, to confirm the back-lean
  direction (taken from where the bar sits in the rack) holds on real video.
- **Common mix-ups.** An overhead press uploaded as a squat or bench, push presses (leg drive), and RDLs
  filmed from slightly angled views, to check lift detection and the side-view requirement.

## Assumptions

- **Lower-back arch is approximated by backward torso lean.** A pose has no spine points. "Back" is away
  from the lifter's front, taken from the bar sitting in front of the shoulders in the rack. A lifter who
  racks the bar behind the neck (a behind-the-neck press) would read the wrong way; this is out of scope.
- **The RDL is counted from the hinge.** Its hips barely drop (0.01 in the synthetic set, against the 0.05
  minimum used for squat and deadlift reps), so it uses the shoulder-hip-knee angle.
- **Lift detection.**
  - Arms locked out overhead is distinctive, so a press uploaded as another lift is analyzed, and
    validated, as an overhead press.
  - Pose alone can't tell an RDL from a conventional deadlift, so the selected lift wins, as it does for
    squat and deadlift.
  - Lying down still means bench, as before.
- **Both lifts need a side view,** like most existing faults. From any other view every fault is listed as
  "needs a side view", as for the side-view faults of the existing lifts.
- **Fault IDs repeat across lifts,** for example `bar_path_drift` and `incomplete_lockout`. Fault IDs are
  per lift, so each lift keeps its own name, threshold and joints for them.
- **The US-02 criterion wasn't rewritten.** "Exactly three options" was the Alpha scope, so it is kept with
  a note that US-28 superseded it.
