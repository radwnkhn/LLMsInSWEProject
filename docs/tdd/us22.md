# US-22: Blind trainer grading

Issue: [#24](https://github.com/radwnkhn/LLMsInSWEProject/issues/24).
Branch `beta/us-22-trainer-grading` starts from `main` (`059a3f9`), which includes US-19 to US-21 and
US-25 to US-28. It is independent of US-24. US-23 (the metrics dashboard) builds on this branch, because
trainer agreement comes from these grades.

and `issues/create_github_issues/issues.py` (`Add US-22 acceptance criteria ...`). The tests were then

The 39 errors are per-test setup failures, not collection errors. Trainer accounts didn't exist yet

endpoints returning 404 instead of 401. They also show that Supabase's `role: "trainer"` and test-mode
`@trainer.test` sign-ups weren't recognized. The browser test times out looking for the Trainer grading

All tests were AI-generated. None are marked manual; a team member should review and refine them as

## What changed

- **Trainer accounts.**
  - `AuthUser.is_trainer` comes from Supabase `app_metadata.role = "trainer"`, set like the admin role.
  - In test mode, sign-ups at `@trainer.test` become trainers (`TEST_TRAINER_DOMAIN`).
  - `public.users.is_trainer` is saved on each request, as `is_admin` already was.
  - Sign-up, login and `/auth/me` report `is_trainer`.
- **`backend/app/grading.py`.**
  - Eligibility: finished, validated analyses (passed first try or after a retry), never already assigned to
    that trainer, never the trainer's own, optionally of one lift.
  - Random sampling.
  - Verdict and status labels.
  - What a trainer may see of our feedback: top fix, cue, explanation and other faults; no measurements or
    lifter details.
- **Repository.**
  - `list_trainers`, `create_assignments` (skips duplicates), `list_assignments` (newest first),
    `get_assignment`.
  - `save_trainer_answer` (once only) and `save_verdict` (only after an answer), in memory and in Postgres.
- **Migration `20261116000000_us22_trainer_grading.sql`.**
  - `users.is_trainer`, and `public.grading_assignments` with one row per analysis and trainer.
  - Check constraints keep the order: answer before grade.
  - Deleting the analysis or the trainer cascades.
  - RLS lets a trainer read only their own rows and admins all rows. Trainers get no read access to
    analyses, uploads or users.
- **API.**
  - Admins: `GET /admin/trainers`, `POST /admin/grading/assignments` (`trainer_id`, `count` 1 to 50,
    optional `lift`; 409 when nothing is left) and `GET /admin/grading/assignments`.
  - Trainers: `GET /trainer/assignments` and `GET /trainer/assignments/{id}`.
  - `POST .../answer` takes `fault_id`, or null for "No major issue", and answers once (409 after that).
  - `POST .../verdict` gives 409 until the trainer's own answer is saved.
  - A `trainer_user` dependency returns 403 for non-trainers. Another trainer's assignment returns 404.
- **Frontend.**
  - `/admin/grading`, linked from the Analyses admin page, has an "Assign a sample" form (trainer, number,
    lift) and the assignments table.
  - `/grading` lists a trainer's sets.
  - `/grading/[id]` shows the video and the lift's faults. After "Save my answer" it shows our feedback and
    the Correct / Partly correct / Wrong grade.
  - `/grading` is a signed-in page in the middleware.

## Assumptions

- **What gets graded.** Only analyses that passed validation have coaching feedback to grade. A "No major
  issues found" result is gradable too: the trainer can agree that nothing needed fixing.
- **Blind means before our feedback.** The trainer sees the video and the lift (which they could see anyway),
  but not our top fix, cue, other faults, rep count or measurements until their own answer is saved. The
  answer then can't change. The grade can be changed later, since by then it's no longer blind.
- **Fault choices** are the lift's enabled faults when the trainer opens the set, plus "No major issue".
- **Privacy.** Trainer responses never include the lifter's email, note or file name. The signed video link
  contains storage IDs (account and upload UUIDs), but no name or email.
- **Trainers list.** A trainer appears in the admin's list once they have signed in after getting the role,
  because that's when the role is saved to `public.users`.
- **Sampling is random per request** (`random.SystemRandom`) and never repeats an analysis for the same
  trainer. Two trainers may get the same analysis, which lets their grades be compared later.
- **Navigation.** The nav bar is the same for everyone. Trainers open `/grading`, which an admin can share.
